What do these result codes mean?
  • Supported — the cited source states or logically implies the claim.
  • Partially supported — same topic and right direction, but the claim adds specifics not in the source.
  • Not supported — the source contains nothing relevant to the claim.
  • Contradicted — the source states the opposite of the claim.
  • Cited article doesn't exist — the citation does not resolve to a real, retrievable document.
  • Could not access — the article exists but its content could not be retrieved or is too sparse to assess.
